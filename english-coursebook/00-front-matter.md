## A Complete English Coursebook for Intermediate Learners

---

# Preface

Welcome to *English Bridge: From A2 to B1*. This book is designed for learners who know some English but want to improve. If you can understand simple conversations, talk about your daily life, and read short texts — but you still find it difficult to express your ideas clearly — this book is for you.

This coursebook follows the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). It takes you step by step from A2 (basic) to B1 (intermediate). Each unit builds on the previous one, so your English grows naturally.

## How to Use This Book

Each unit has 10 sections:

| Section | What You Will Do |
|---------|------------------|
| **Introduction** | Learn about the unit theme |
| **Vocabulary** | Learn new words and phrases |
| **Grammar** | Study important grammar rules |
| **Reading** | Read a text and understand it |
| **Comprehension** | Check your understanding |
| **Listening** | Practice listening with a script |
| **Speaking** | Talk with a partner |
| **Writing** | Write about the topic |
| **Pronunciation** | Improve your speaking |
| **Review** | Test what you learned |

## Tips for Learning

1. **Study regularly.** Try to study for 20-30 minutes every day.
2. **Review old units.** Go back to previous units to remember vocabulary and grammar.
3. **Speak out loud.** Reading is not enough. Say the words and sentences aloud.
4. **Write full answers.** When you answer questions, write complete sentences.
5. **Use a notebook.** Write new words, grammar rules, and your own examples.
